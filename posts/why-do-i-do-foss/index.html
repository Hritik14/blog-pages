Why do I do FOSS ?

So, why do FOSS when I’m not paid for it ?

I’ve been asked this several times - by others and by myself included. One of my collegues went as far as to say - “you only like to do things for free and donation, you must not like money at all.”

I assure you, this is not the case. I love money.

My job⌗

The job I currently have, is because of FOSS. Since, I had quality contributions, getting a job in my domain was not difficult. Before I was a security engineer, I was a backend python developer. FOSS helped me to write better code and think more critically.

I got mentors like @pombreddane. I couldn’t pay for it monitarily, so I gave what I could - time.

After getting a job I like, my foss activity has gone down significantly. It’s as if I used FOSS to get a job and that’s it.

The Ego⌗

It feels good to say - I’m a maintainer of XYZ. It’s just cool.

Future⌗

Something pulls be back to FOSS, probably it’s my future. Since I got my job via FOSS, I might grow further via FOSS as well, who knows ?

Why not get a job in FOSS ?⌗

As of Thu Oct 23 02:10:16 IST 2025, I don’t have an answer to this. I could get a job in a FOSS based company. It might give me a fully remote job and I might be able to move out of this city to somewhere better. Although, it would be a job nonetheless. I want to build something and be a part of it. Axilock could be that something - or even 0daylabs. If either of this does not show any promise by end of this year, I should change my strategy.

The qualiy of people I’ve found in FOSS has typically been much better than in closed source jobs I’ve had. Perhaps, I’ve not had very good jobs. After getting a job, you could pretty much slack off and things still go the way they’re supposed to go. The machinary is large. Getting the job is also not very competency related everywhere. Sometimes it’s the connections, sometimes it’s the competitive programming challenges - both the things I’m very bad at. With FOSS, you could target the best of best projects and there’s no gatekeeping - neither connection nor weird puzzle solving is required.

So, do I do FOSS to build good connections ? If so, why don’t i get a job in FOSS after building good connections ?
